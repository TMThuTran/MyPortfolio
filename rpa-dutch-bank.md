## The situation

A large Dutch bank wanted to reduce the manual effort in processing customer requests, and to make that processing more accurate. Robotic process automation (RPA) let software bots take over repetitive steps across the bank's existing systems.

## What I did

- **Designed, built and deployed 11 RPA solutions,** from analysing the process to running the bots in production.
- **Grew the team's capability.** I helped expand Accenture's Automation Anywhere team: nine developers got certified within a year, I added to the internal knowledge base, and I organised four training sessions.
- **Mentored a thesis intern** through a six-month virtual internship on the RPA and natural language processing landscape. The thesis passed with distinction.

## The result

The bots freed up the equivalent of 11 full-time roles and increased accuracy in processing customer requests by 20%. One of the solutions won the bank's Cross Product Tooling Tribe award for the best innovative solution of the month, and I was selected for Accenture Heerlen's 2021 talent programme.

## What I'm taking into AI products

RPA taught me where automation really earns its keep: choosing the right process matters more than building the bot. A process that changes every month or depends on judgement will break a rule-based bot, and the bot breaks loudly. AI agents can handle more of that judgement, but they fail quietly instead. The lesson I carry forward is to pick problems where the value is real and the failures can be caught, and to build the catching in from the start.
