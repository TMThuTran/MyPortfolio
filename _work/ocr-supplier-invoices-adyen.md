## The situation

Adyen's finance team was booking supplier invoices with a lot of manual data entry. As the company grew, that work grew with it, and it slowed down the purchase-to-pay process.
<!-- Add any context you're comfortable sharing publicly: rough invoice volumes, team size, how long booking used to take. -->

## What I did

- **Led the implementation** of a cloud-based OCR solution in partnership with an external vendor, from requirements to go-live.
- **Defined the integration requirements.** I worked with security and Workday specialists to specify the APIs, so engineers could build the connection between the OCR solution and Adyen's finance systems.
- **Fitted the tool to the process,** not the other way round: deciding where extracted data could flow straight through and where a person needed to check it.

## The result

Supplier invoice booking time dropped by 30%, and the purchase-to-pay process became more robust.

## What I'm taking into AI products

OCR is machine learning in a very practical form: every extracted field comes with a level of confidence, and some of them are wrong. Making it work in finance was never only about the model. It was about deciding which errors were acceptable, routing the uncertain cases to people, and making it easy for them to correct the output. That's the same set of decisions an AI product manager makes today about language models, just with higher stakes and more ambiguity.
