I've spent eight years at the point where finance and banking processes meet software. At Accenture I built robotic process automation for a large Dutch bank. At Adyen I led an OCR rollout for supplier invoices. At Yokoy I ran expense-software implementations end to end. Today I'm a business analyst at Rabobank, working with product owners, designers and engineers on the Rabo Business Banking platform.

Along the way I learned that automation rarely fails on the happy path. It fails on exceptions, on edge cases nobody wrote down, and on people who don't trust what the system did. That's the part of the work I care most about, and it's exactly where AI agents are now being asked to perform.

So I'm deliberately building my AI product skills: working through the craft of product discovery alongside the engineering of AI systems, from model architectures to evaluation. My current [side project]({{ '/projects/agentic-invoice-reconciliation/' | relative_url }}) is an agentic approach to invoice reconciliation, a problem I know from the inside.
